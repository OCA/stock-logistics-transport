Viewing Routes
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

1. Navigate to TMS > Orders
2. Select an order with delivery stops
3. Click on the "Map" button or use the map view

The map displays:

- Numbered markers for each delivery stop
- Blue polyline showing the optimized route
- Sidebar with total distance and estimated time
- Click on a stop to center the map

Navigation
~~~~~~~~~~

Click on any stop marker to open the popup with:

- Stop address and details
- "Navigate" button to open Google Maps directions
