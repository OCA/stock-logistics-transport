1. Confirm the shipment advice and, if needed, plan its transfers.
1. Press *Send by Email* to open the composer with the *Shipment Advice: Send by
   Email* template and the shipment advice report attached.
1. Select the recipients manually: a shipment advice can group pickings for
   several customers, so no default recipients are set.
1. Send the email. The message is posted in the shipment advice chatter.

The email body lists the planned pickings with their origin, delivery address,
weight and number of products. The attached report shows the loading details
registered at the moment of sending.
