## Open the planner

1. Open a cargo trip whose vehicle has spaces.
2. Click **Load planner**.

The truck shows each space. A booked space is a solid cube. An available
space is a wireframe. A blocked space is grey. The sidebar lists three
groups: cargo already in a space, cargo on the trip with no space, and
cargo that is not on a trip yet.

## Select a cargo

Click a cube or a card. The space is outlined in yellow on the truck, and
the matching card is highlighted in the sidebar. The panel shows the cargo
state, the space, the pieces, the weight, the volume, and whether the cargo
is stackable.

## Move a cargo

Drag a cargo onto another space of this vehicle. The previous allocation
becomes available, and the new one becomes booked. A cargo that is already
on the trip can change space after the trip has started.

Drag a cargo onto **On the trip, no space** to clear its space and keep it
on the trip.

Drag a card from **Unassigned cargo** onto a space to put that cargo on the
trip.

A space above cargo that is not stackable stays blocked. A blocked vehicle
space cannot be booked on an open trip of that vehicle.
